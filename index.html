Baca Grande Parcel Maps

Two maps of the Baca Grande lots in Crestone, Colorado, built from public Saguache County records.

Parcel Map Original: the county parcel viewer, as the county publishes it.

Parcel Map Evolved: the same parcels with Baca questions, plat lines, flood, wildfire and terrain layers. It is large and takes a moment to load.
